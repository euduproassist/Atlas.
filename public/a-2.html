Application Information

Before you apply, please ensure you have the following required documentation ready:

ID Document

Clear copy of your valid ID or Passport.

Academic Results

Your latest Grade 12 or tertiary results.

Proof of Payment

Application fee receipt (if applicable).

Other Documents

Additional study certificates if requested.

  • Certified copy of your National Senior Certificate or Equivalent.
  • Certified copy of South African ID or International Passport.
  • Academic Transcripts if transferring from another university.
  • Valid Email Address and Mobile Phone Number for OTP verification.
